Copy trading allows you to automatically replicate the trades of experienced traders. It has become one of the most popular features on modern crypto exchanges.

## What Is Copy Trading?

Copy trading lets you connect your account to a lead trader and automatically mirror their positions. When the lead trader opens a trade, the same trade is executed in your account proportionally. **Bitget** pioneered this feature with its **One-Click Copy Trade** system, and **Bybit** and **Binance** offer similar functionality.

## How to Choose a Trader to Copy

Selecting the right lead trader is critical. Key metrics to evaluate:

- **Total P&L** — Overall profitability
- **Win rate** — Percentage of profitable trades
- **Total followers** — Social proof of performance
- **Risk score** — Measures trading aggressiveness
- **Copying amount** — How much capital others have allocated

## Benefits and Risks

The main benefit of copy trading is access to professional strategies without spending years learning technical analysis. However, past performance does not guarantee future results, and you can lose money even when following a profitable trader if you enter at the wrong time.

## Risk Management Tips

When copy trading, allocate only a portion of your trading capital, diversify across multiple lead traders, and monitor performance regularly. Set appropriate stop-loss limits and don't invest more than you're comfortable losing.

## Conclusion

Copy trading is an excellent way to learn from experienced traders while staying active in the market. Choose your lead traders carefully and manage your risk exposure wisely.
