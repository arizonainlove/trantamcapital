Cryptocurrency exchanges offer multiple trading modes. Understanding the difference between spot and futures trading is essential before committing capital.

## Spot Trading Explained

Spot trading involves buying and selling cryptocurrencies for immediate delivery. When you buy **1 BTC** on a spot market, you own **1 BTC**. It's straightforward: you own the actual asset.

| Aspect | Spot Trading | Futures Trading |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Ownership | Own the asset | Contract only |
| Leverage | None (1x) | Up to **100x** |
| Risk | Limited to investment | Can exceed deposit |
| Complexity | Low | High |

## Futures Trading Explained

Futures trading allows you to speculate on price movements without owning the underlying asset. **Bybit** and **Binance** offer robust futures markets with features like isolated margin, cross-margin, and various order types.

Futures trading introduces **leverage**, which amplifies both gains and losses. A **10x leveraged** position means a **10%** price movement results in a **100%** gain or loss of your margin.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Spot trading** is ideal for beginners, long-term holders, and those who want to actually own cryptocurrency. **Futures trading** suits experienced traders who understand leverage risk and want to hedge or speculate.

## Risk Management

Regardless of your choice, proper risk management is crucial. Never risk more than you can afford to lose, use stop-loss orders, and start with small positions when learning new trading instruments.

## Conclusion

Both spot and futures trading have their place in a trader's toolkit. Start with spot trading to learn the basics, then explore futures as your experience grows.

*Disclaimer: Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss. Only trade with capital you can afford to lose.*
