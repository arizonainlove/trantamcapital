Blue Guardian is a proprietary trading firm that offers funded trading accounts to traders who pass a structured evaluation process. Founded around 2021–2022, the firm has grown rapidly and now supports traders in over 160 countries.

The firm's standout feature is its profit split of up to **90%**, which is among the highest in the prop trading industry. Combined with affordable challenge fees starting from around **$50** and a advertised 24-hour payout guarantee, Blue Guardian has attracted a large and active trader community.

### Trading Platforms and Instruments

Blue Guardian supports four major trading platforms: **MT5**, **Match-Trader**, **TradeLocker**, and **DXtrade**. This wide selection gives traders flexibility in choosing their preferred environment and allows EA usage and scalping strategies.

The available instruments cover forex pairs, gold, major indices, commodities, and crypto CFDs. This range is sufficient for most retail prop traders focused on traditional markets.

### Account Types

The firm offers four main account types:

| Account Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Instant Funding** | Get funded immediately without evaluation |
| **1-Step Challenge** | Single-phase evaluation, faster to pass |
| **2-Step Challenge** | Two-phase evaluation, most popular choice |
| **3-Step Challenge** | Three-phase evaluation, lowest entry cost |

Each account type has specific rules regarding profit targets, daily loss limits, and maximum drawdown. Traders should carefully review these before choosing.

### Affiliate Program

Blue Guardian's affiliate program is a notable strength. It offers recurring commissions, fast affiliate payouts, and support for crypto withdrawals. Affiliates also receive a free funded account after their first successful referral, making the program attractive for content creators and review sites.

### Considerations

While Blue Guardian offers compelling terms, traders should be aware of several important factors. The firm operates without traditional financial regulation, meaning there is no external investor protection. Community feedback is mixed — while many traders report successful payouts, others have experienced delays or difficulties.

The trading rules can be complex, with strict consistency requirements and drawdown limits. Beginners without solid risk management experience may find the evaluation process challenging.
