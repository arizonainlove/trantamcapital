Exchange D is a decentralized exchange (DEX) that prioritizes user sovereignty and self-custody of funds. By operating without a central authority, they eliminate counterparty risk and allow users to retain full control over their assets at all times.

The platform supports cross-chain swaps and offers competitive gas fees through optimized smart contract architecture. While the DEX model provides superior security, it may present a learning curve for beginners who are accustomed to centralized exchange interfaces. The exchange is community-governed, with token holders voting on protocol upgrades and parameters.
