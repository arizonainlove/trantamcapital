Bitcoin has reached a historic milestone, surging past the $100,000 mark for the first time in its history. The flagship cryptocurrency hit the six-figure milestone during Asian trading hours on Wednesday.

## Key Drivers

Several factors have contributed to Bitcoin's remarkable rally:

- **Institutional Adoption**: Major financial institutions have continued to increase their Bitcoin exposure
- **ETF Inflows**: Spot Bitcoin ETFs have seen record inflows throughout the year
- **Regulatory Clarity**: Clearer regulatory frameworks in multiple jurisdictions
- **Halving Effect**: The 2024 halving has reduced supply pressure

## Market Impact

The surge past $100K has triggered a broader rally across the cryptocurrency market, with Ethereum, Solana, and other major altcoins posting significant gains.

## What Analysts Say

Market analysts remain bullish on Bitcoin's trajectory, with many projecting further gains in the coming months. However, they caution that volatility remains high and corrections are to be expected after such a rapid ascent.

## Looking Ahead

As Bitcoin enters price discovery territory above $100K, all eyes are on whether it can sustain these levels and establish a new trading range. Key support levels will be crucial in determining the next direction.
