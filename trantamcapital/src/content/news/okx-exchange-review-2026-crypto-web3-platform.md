## OKX's Weaknesses

### Steep Learning Curve for Beginners

With so many features spanning futures, Web3, DEX, DeFi, and bots, new users may find the platform overwhelming at first.

### Increasing KYC and Regulation

Like all major exchanges, OKX has tightened KYC requirements and restricted certain jurisdictions — an industry-wide trend as crypto regulation evolves.

### Spot Volume Trails Binance

While OKX's futures trading is very strong, its spot trading liquidity, volume, and user base have not yet surpassed the market leader. Users who need deep spot liquidity may prefer [Binance](/crypto-exchange/exchange-a).

### Customer Support Delays

During periods of high market volatility, support tickets and live chat can be slow to respond. This is a common challenge across high-volume exchanges.
