Today, Bitget is best known for its copy trading platform, competitive fees, deep futures liquidity, early altcoin listings, and generous affiliate program. But is it trustworthy and right for you? Let's find out.

## Bitget's Key Strengths

This is Bitget's standout feature. Users can copy professional traders, review their performance and history, and manage risk on copied positions.

## Bitget's Weaknesses

### Spot Liquidity

While futures and copy trading are top-tier, Bitget's spot trading volume trails the market leader. Users who prioritize deep spot order books may prefer larger platforms.

### Leverage Risk

Bitget supports leverage up to **125x**. While this offers high profit potential, liquidation risk is equally significant. Beginners must practice strict risk management.

### KYC Requirements

Like all major exchanges, Bitget has tightened its KYC policies, restricting access in certain jurisdictions.

### Customer Support

During periods of high volatility, support tickets and live chat can experience delays — a common issue across the industry.
