## Introduction

In recent years, **Bybit** has grown into one of the fastest-growing cryptocurrency exchanges worldwide. Founded in **2018** by Ben Zhou, the platform initially focused on crypto futures, perpetual contracts, and derivatives trading.

Today, Bybit has expanded into spot trading, copy trading, Launchpad, trading bots, Earn & Staking, Web3 wallet, NFT marketplace, and AI trading tools. It now ranks among the largest futures exchanges globally with top-tier liquidity and a strong presence in Asia.

This is Bybit's biggest advantage. The exchange is known for deep order books, fast execution, low slippage, and stable funding rates.

Liquidity is strong across **BTC**, **ETH**, and altcoin futures. Bybit is particularly well-suited for:

## Bybit's Weaknesses

### Spot Trading Trails Binance

While Bybit's futures trading is exceptional, its spot trading volume and coin selection have not yet surpassed the market leader. Users who need deep spot liquidity may prefer [Binance](/crypto-exchange/binance).

### Futures Risk for Beginners

Bybit excels at leveraged trading, which carries substantial risk. Beginners may overtrade, over-leverage, or lose their accounts during volatile markets without proper risk management.

### Geographic Restrictions

Due to regulatory concerns, Bybit restricts access in certain countries. KYC requirements have also become stricter over time — a common trend across major crypto exchanges.

### Customer Support Delays

During periods of high market volatility, support tickets and live chat can be slow to respond. This is a widely shared challenge among high-volume crypto exchanges.
