## What Is Blue Guardian?

Blue Guardian is a proprietary trading firm that provides funded trading accounts to traders who pass a challenge evaluation. The firm has been active since approximately **2021–2022** and supports traders in over **160 countries** with account sizes up to **$400,000**.

## Blue Guardian's Key Strengths

### High Profit Split

One of Blue Guardian's most attractive features is its profit split of up to **90%**. This means traders keep the vast majority of their profits, making it more favorable than many smaller prop firms.

### Fast Payouts

Blue Guardian markets a **24-hour payout guarantee**. Many traders confirm that smaller withdrawal requests are processed quickly, typically within **24–48 hours**. This has helped the firm build a strong reputation in the prop trading community.

### Multiple Trading Platforms

Blue Guardian supports a wide range of platforms:

- **MT5**
- **Match-Trader**
- **TradeLocker**
- **DXtrade**

This variety gives traders flexibility, makes EA (Expert Advisor) usage easier, and allows scalping strategies.

### Affordable Challenge Fees

Blue Guardian frequently offers discounts and coupons on challenge fees. Entry costs are generally lower than many competitors, with some accounts costing as little as a few tens of dollars to start.

### Attractive Affiliate Program

Blue Guardian offers recurring commissions, fast affiliate payouts, and crypto payout support. Affiliates also receive a free funded account after their first referral. This makes it appealing for bloggers, YouTubers, and prop firm review websites.
