  ## Binance Weaknesses

  ### 1. Regulatory Scrutiny

  Binance's massive scale has made it a target for regulators worldwide, leading to investigations in multiple jurisdictions, service restrictions in certain countries, and
  evolving KYC requirements.

  ### 2. Customer Support Delays

  During periods of high market volatility, users may experience slower ticket response times and congested live chat queues — a common pain point across major crypto
  exchanges.

  ### 3. Futures Trading Risk

  Binance Futures offers high leverage that can amplify losses as easily as gains. Altcoin futures are particularly volatile. New traders should start with low leverage, use
  stop-losses, and learn proper position sizing.

  ### 4. Steep Learning Curve

  The sheer breadth of features can overwhelm new users. However, once the learning curve is overcome, this complexity becomes a significant advantage — most traders never need
   another platform.
