## 6. TRANG FOR BEGINNERS

- **Page Title:** "For Beginners"
- **Subtitle:** "Start your trading journey with our comprehensive beginner guides"

### Understanding the Markets (3 cards)

1. **What is Cryptocurrency?**
   - "Digital or virtual currency that uses cryptography for security. Cryptocurrencies operate on decentralized networks based on blockchain technology, making them resistant to government interference or manipulation."

2. **What is Forex Trading?**
   - "The foreign exchange (forex) market is the global marketplace for trading national currencies. With $7.5 trillion in daily volume, it's the largest and most liquid financial market in the world."

3. **What are Binary Options?**
   - "Binary options are financial instruments that allow you to speculate on the price movement of an asset. You predict whether the price will be above or below a certain level at a specific time."

### Start Trading in 7 Steps

1. **Choose Your Market** — "Decide which market (forex, crypto, or binary options) aligns with your risk tolerance and interests."
2. **Select a Broker** — "Research and choose a regulated broker with competitive fees and reliable customer support."
3. **Open an Account** — "Sign up with your chosen broker, complete the verification process, and fund your account."
4. **Learn the Basics** — "Study market analysis, trading strategies, risk management, and platform features."
5. **Start with a Demo** — "Practice with virtual funds to build confidence and test your strategies without financial risk."
6. **Develop a Strategy** — "Create a trading plan with entry and exit rules, risk management parameters, and profit targets."
7. **Go Live** — "Start trading with real money, beginning with small positions and gradually increasing as you gain experience."

### Trading Glossary (15 terms)

1. **Ask Price** — "The lowest price a seller is willing to accept for an asset."
2. **Bid Price** — "The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for an asset."
3. **Spread** — "The difference between the ask price and the bid price."
4. **Leverage** — "Using borrowed capital to increase the potential return of an investment."
5. **Margin** — "The amount of money required to open and maintain a leveraged position."
6. **Pip** — "The smallest price movement in a currency pair, typically 0.0001 for most pairs."
7. **Volatility** — "The rate at which the price of an asset increases or decreases over time."
8. **Liquidity** — "The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold without affecting its price."
9. **Bull Market** — "A market condition where prices are rising or expected to rise."
10. **Bear Market** — "A market condition where prices are falling or expected to fall."
11. **Stop Loss** — "An order placed to automatically sell an asset when it reaches a specific price to limit losses."
12. **Take Profit** — "An order placed to automatically sell an asset when it reaches a specific profit target."
13. **Diversification** — "Spreading investments across different assets to reduce risk."
14. **Technical Analysis** — "Analyzing price charts and market data to forecast future price movements."
15. **Fundamental Analysis** — "Evaluating economic factors and news events to determine an asset's intrinsic value."
